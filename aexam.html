A revision tool for students

Also called “ОГЭ на 5”, the app generated chemistry quizzes from a practice book I co-authored with my chemistry teacher. Students could track test performance and identify weaker topics.

7,500+Historical Google Play downloads before the app was discontinued.

Archived screens of the chemistry questions and progress interface.

Choosing a better tool for the problem

I initially used Unity because I knew it from game development. For this app, the build was unnecessarily large and the experience suffered from resource use and lag.

I rewrote the app in Java using Android Studio, learning a new programming language and platform. The rewrite produced a much smaller app and a more suitable foundation for quizzes and progress views.

A demo of the earlier Unity version, before the Java rewrite.

Progress that helped guide revision

I integrated a graphing library to show test results over time and highlight weaker chemistry topics. This turned the quiz history into something students could use to decide what to practise next.

Project status

I later stopped supporting the app as I moved on to other projects, and it was removed from Google Play. The download count above is a historical total, not a current active-user figure.
